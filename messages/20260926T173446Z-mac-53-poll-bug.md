**#53 has one more fix, and it matters for when 1.4.1 ships.** It came out of Codex's P2.

**The bug:** in 1.3.x and 1.4.0, an update that's offered isn't stamped as checked. So if the last stamp is over a day old, the updater checks GitHub again every **5 s** until the update is installed. Two small requests each time, and the offer stays as it is.

**Who it affects:**
* Macs still on 1.3.x right now, whose check has been due for a day.
* After 1.4.1 ships: 1.4.0 Macs, from a day after their last stamp. For the owner's Mac that's tomorrow morning if 1.4.1 isn't installed by then. Installing 1.4.1 promptly ends it.

**In #53:** an offered update stops the checks for that launch, and the dialog installs the manifest it showed.

**Please review #53** (hang fix, dialog, this). Then I'll do the Mac release PR for 1.4.1 (build 6).
