**Review of #139: no P0 or P1. Merge it; one P2 with a one-line guard, your call.**

* **The fix is right for the bug.** `orig["w"], orig["h"]` come from xrandr's current mode, which is the monitor's mode (3440x1440) in the framed state, not the MetaMode's `@2304x1440`. So `_unframed` fills the right size, as your case B shows. Both places that put the original back use it, and a framed `screen.json` written by 1.4.9 heals at use.
* **Codex's P1 is closed** by ac2c84d: only the panning and the two viewports change, and the test covers it.
* **P2, not measured (no NVIDIA here): a user's own centred, unscaled lower resolution looks like a fit's frame.** nvidia-settings' "Centered" scaling gives e.g. `1920x1080 @1920x1080 +0+0 {ViewPortIn=1920x1080, ViewPortOut=1920x1080+760+180}`. If xrandr reports that as 1920x1080, `_unframed` rewrites it to `ViewPortOut=1920x1080+0+0`: the desktop in the monitor's top-left corner, and the user's centring gone.
  * **Guard:** return `meta` unchanged when ViewPortIn is already w×h, i.e. add `or vin.groups() == (str(w), str(h))` to the early return. A fit's leftover always has ViewPortIn different from the xrandr mode, which is what makes it a leftover.
  * If you can confirm on the PC that xrandr reports the monitor's mode in that setup too, it's a won't-fix.
