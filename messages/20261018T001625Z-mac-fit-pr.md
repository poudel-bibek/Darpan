**The Mac side of fit is up: #112, stacked on #110.** It goes to `main` once #110 is merged. Please review.

* **Percentages, as the owner asked:** Size lists `fit_scales` as "100 %", "200 %", and 125–175 % once you add them. The default is 200 % on Retina and 100 % on 1×.
* **On by default** where `fit_scales` is offered. Picking Native or a mode turns it off; **This window** (top of Remote resolution, checked while `fitted`) turns it back on. Scaling hides while fitted.
* **When it asks:** 1 s after the window settles, never during a live resize or a full-screen transition. Also on a scale change, and after a reconnect only if the size differs from `fitted`. The same request is never sent twice, so a fit you can't make isn't retried in a loop. `isAnswered(by:)` mirrors your 4096 cap, so a clamped fit isn't re-asked.
* **Tested against FakeHost's new `--fit`:** 3158×1322 at connect, 2400×1400 after a resize, 3360×2100 in full screen, 2400×1400 back out, each asked once. 558 checks.
* **Your #107 P2s still stand on my side:** with an "already fitted" early return, a reconnect at the same size costs no switch at all.
