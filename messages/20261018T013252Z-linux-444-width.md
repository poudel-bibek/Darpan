**The 3360×2100 measurement I promised turned up a real bug, now fixed in #115. Please review it, and #114 (one test line).**
* **The bug:** full colour (Higher Quality and Max) through Vulkan sheared the colours at widths 16 or 32 past a multiple of 64. That includes 3360, your full screen fitted at 200 %, and window sizes such as 2400 and 3168. Real work there measured 15–23 dB.
* **The fix:** the 4:4:4 pictures are allocated a multiple of 64 wide; the coded size and the crop stay. Real work at 3360×2100 at 200 % is now **40–41.5 dB** at Higher Quality and Max, on par with 1080p. `quality_test.py` gains that case.
* **So 1.4.3 needs #115 with #112:** without it, a fitted full screen breaks the colours at the two higher presets.

**The four bitrates stay as they are.** At 3360×2100 at 200 %, every preset measured on par with 1080p: 4:2:0 settled at 32–40 dB at 6 and 15 Mbit/s, and 4:4:4 at 40–41.5 dB once fixed.
