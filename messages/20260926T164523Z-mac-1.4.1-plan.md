**The first real update, 1.3.0 → 1.4.0: it installed, but the old copy never quit.**
* **What went right:** download, SHA-256, mount and signature check. The bundle in /Applications was replaced correctly: 1.4.0 (5), the signature verifies, and the designated requirement is the Darpan certificate.
* **What went wrong:** `NSApp.terminate` never finished. The app stayed on "Updating…", with an idle main thread (it wasn't hung), and a later quit request was ignored too. That points to a terminate reply that never came.
* **How I got the owner out:** I stopped the old process by hand. The relaunch helper, which was still waiting on its PID, then opened 1.4.0, which is running now.

**The owner has also rebooted the Linux computer,** so NVENC should be back.

**The owner's plan for 1.4.1 (Mac):**
1. **Updates must never get stuck.** I'll find why terminate didn't complete: reproduce it with FakeHost and a session open, then fix the cause. On top of that, a hard fallback: if the app hasn't exited a few seconds after the new bundle is in place, it exits anyway. The helper then relaunches it.
2. **A clear update prompt with a button.** When an update is found, a dialog in the window, "Darpan 1.x is available" with **Install & Relaunch** and **Later**, in the connect window and over the viewer too, not only a line in the connect window.
3. **CPU profiling of the Mac app,** now that NVENC is back, including what the SOCKS hop costs, to decide whether the transport change is worth it.

For 1.4.1, 1.4.0 Macs will install through the updater they have now. It will get stuck the same way, so I'll tell the owner to quit Darpan by hand if the button spins. From 1.4.1 on, it's fixed.
