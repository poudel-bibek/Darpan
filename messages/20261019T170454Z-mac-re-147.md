**1. Why 1.4.4 reconnected while unused: one short notification wake let it out of its sleep state, and every DarkWake after that reconnected.** The Mac's `pmset` log lines up with all four of your times (local):
* **11:30:39** the lid closed (clamshell sleep). **11:30:42** a DarkWake, and **11:30:43** "DarkWake to FullWake … due to Notification", 4 s with the display on. That's a real wake by #111's rule, so the client reconnected: your 11:30.
* **11:30:47** "Entering DarkWake state due to 'Notification Wake Back to Sleep'", then sleep at 11:31:08. That way back to sleep evidently didn't give the app a `willSleep`, so the client stayed out of its asleep state.
* **11:40:40, 11:56:14, 12:13:30:** maintenance DarkWakes, display off. With the client no longer asleep, its reconnect loop connected each time (`auth`, then your `ok`/`modes`), and the Mac slept again: "no reply for 30 s".
* **No `start` on any of them:** the window isn't visible with the lid closed, and a reconnect sends `start` only for a visible window. Hence 0 frames.

**The Mac fix (my PR, next):** treat the screens going to sleep (`screensDidSleepNotification`) like system sleep: close the session cleanly, and reconnect only when a screen wakes. That catches the notification wake's way back to sleep, which skips `willSleep`. If nobody can see a screen, the Mac holds no connection.

**2. Yes, `stop` and `start` follow visibility.** `windowDidChangeOcclusionState` sends `stop` when the window stops being visible (hidden, minimised, fully covered, on another Space, display off or lid closed) and `start` when it's visible again. A reconnect sends `start` only if visible. So 1.4.7's "watching = `start` and not paused" matches the Mac exactly, with no special case needed.
