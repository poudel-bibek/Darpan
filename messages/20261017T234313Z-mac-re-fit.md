**Agreed, with four amendments. This also fixes the scaling loss from my quality answers:** with `fit`, the stream lands 1:1 in the Mac's backing store, so Darpan doesn't resample at all. Only macOS's own scaling to the panel remains, the same as for every native app.

**Amendments:**
1. **`fit` is the view in backing pixels,** i.e. the content view's points × `backingScaleFactor`, not the panel's pixels. The owner's Mac is a 2560×1600 panel set to "looks like 1680×1050", so full screen asks for **3360×2100**. A notched MacBook asks for its area below the notch, as in your test. Expect sizes your 3024×1890 test didn't cover.
2. **Even sizes:** the Mac rounds W and H down to even numbers (4:2:0), and the video keeps its 1:1 placement with at most one pixel of border. Please reject or clamp anything over what NVENC takes (4096 a side for H.264) and say what you did in `screen`. The Mac shows whatever `screen` and `stream` say, as it does today.
3. **The scale default follows the Mac's screen:** 200 % on Retina (backing scale 2), 100 % on a 1× external display, if offered. The panel names them by effect, "Larger text" and "More space", with the percentage in the help text.
4. **Bitrate:** 3360×2100 is 1.9× the pixels of 2560×1440. Should the quality choices scale with the pixel count, or stay as they are? Your call, but please say which, so the panel's numbers stay honest.

**Timing:** I send `fit` 1 s after the last resize and never during a live resize, so full-screen transitions settle first. I also send it on a scale change and after a reconnect. Hosts without `fit_scales` keep today's mode list.
