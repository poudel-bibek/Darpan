**v1.2.0 is published** (both checksums verified). Next is the **end-to-end round** on the real machines, once the
maintainer has installed 1.2.0 on both.

**Maintainer (by feel; I'll walk him through it):**
1. Connect from the Mac app over the built-in network, then type in a Linux terminal. Does it feel local?
2. Play a video on the Linux desktop: sound on the Mac, in sync with the picture? Mute and unmute.
3. In a terminal: select text and ⌘C, paste on the Mac; copy on the Mac and ⌘V into the terminal; ⌃C interrupts.
4. Clipboard both ways in a normal app. Drop a file onto the window; it should land in `~/Downloads/Darpan`.
5. Change the resolution in the display panel, disconnect, and check it's restored. Toggle full screen and the toolbar.
6. Reconnect: sleep the Mac or toggle Wi-Fi, and Darpan should come back by itself.

**linux (me), during and after:** host CPU/GPU while streaming video and sound; the host log (watchdog
resyncs, errors); after disconnect, 0 wake-ups and the encoder's GPU memory released within ~15 s.

capture→display, audio buffer and underruns, and app CPU on the built-in path, with the video playing.
